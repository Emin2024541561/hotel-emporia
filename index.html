Two Unique Experiences.

Welcome to the Emporia Collection. Every destination tells a story — ours begins among the vineyards of Herzegovina and continues above the rooftops of historic Mostar.

The Emporia Collection brings together two distinctive boutique destinations, united by one philosophy: essence over excess.

Nestled among vineyards and rolling landscapes, Emporia Wine & Dine Hotel celebrates stillness, craftsmanship and the flavours of Herzegovina. In the very heart of Mostar, just moments from the UNESCO-listed Old Bridge, Hotel Boulevard by Emporia offers a contemporary urban retreat where thoughtful design, rooftop wellness, exceptional gastronomy and genuine hospitality come together in perfect harmony.

Together, the two destinations present complementary expressions of the same vision—one immersed in nature, the other embracing the rhythm and cultural richness of the city.

From award-winning wines and carefully crafted cuisine to timeless architecture and immersive experiences, every detail is designed with purpose. Here, refinement is never about excess, but about authenticity, craftsmanship and a profound sense of place.

Discover Mostar from two perspectives.

The tranquillity of the vineyards.

The energy of the city.

One philosophy. One collection. One unmistakable Emporia experience.
